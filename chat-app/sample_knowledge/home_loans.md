# Home Loan Products

## Overview
Our home loan products are designed to help customers achieve their homeownership dreams with competitive rates and flexible terms.

## Home Loan Types

### Fixed Rate Home Loans
- **Interest Rate**: Starting from 5.99% p.a.
- **Loan Term**: Up to 30 years
- **Features**:
  - Rate lock for the entire loan term
  - Predictable monthly payments
  - No surprises with interest rate changes
  - Available for owner-occupied and investment properties

### Variable Rate Home Loans
- **Interest Rate**: Starting from 5.49% p.a.
- **Loan Term**: Up to 30 years
- **Features**:
  - Potentially lower initial rates
  - Rates may decrease with market conditions
  - Extra repayment options without penalties
  - Redraw facility available

### First Home Buyer Loans
- **Interest Rate**: Starting from 5.25% p.a.
- **Loan Term**: Up to 30 years
- **Special Features**:
  - No Lenders Mortgage Insurance (LMI) with 10% deposit
  - Government First Home Owner Grant eligible
  - Reduced establishment fees
  - Free property valuation
  - Dedicated first home buyer support team

## Loan Features

### Offset Account
- 100% offset against your home loan balance
- Access your funds anytime
- Reduces interest charges
- Available on variable rate loans

### Redraw Facility
- Access extra repayments when needed
- No minimum redraw amount
- Online access 24/7
- Helps reduce interest over loan term

### Extra Repayments
- Make additional payments without penalty
- Reduce loan term and total interest
- Flexible payment options
- Can be automated

## Eligibility Requirements

### Income Requirements
- Minimum annual income: $50,000 (individual) or $75,000 (joint)
- Stable employment history (2+ years)
- PAYG, self-employed, or contract income accepted
- Casual income with 12+ months history

### Deposit Requirements
- Minimum 5% deposit (with LMI)
- 10% deposit for LMI waiver (first home buyers)
- 20% deposit to avoid LMI
- Genuine savings or family gifts accepted

### Credit Requirements
- Good credit history required
- Credit score of 600+ preferred
- No recent defaults or bankruptcies
- All existing debts must be disclosed

## Application Process

1. **Pre-approval**: Get conditional approval in 24-48 hours
2. **Property Search**: Shop with confidence knowing your budget
3. **Formal Application**: Submit full documentation
4. **Valuation**: Professional property assessment
5. **Final Approval**: Receive unconditional loan approval
6. **Settlement**: Complete property purchase

## Required Documentation
- Proof of income (payslips, tax returns)
- Bank statements (3 months)
- Asset verification
- Identification documents
- Property purchase contract
- Property information

## Interest Rate Information
Rates are subject to change and depend on:
- Loan amount and loan-to-value ratio
- Property type and location
- Borrower credit profile
- Loan features selected
