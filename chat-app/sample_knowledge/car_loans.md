## Eligibility Requirements

### Income Requirements
- Minimum annual income: $35,000
- Regular employment (6+ months)
- PAYG, self-employed, or pension income accepted
- Proof of income required

### Age Requirements
- Minimum age: 18 years
- Maximum age: 70 years at loan maturity
- Younger borrowers may need guarantor

### Residency Requirements
- Australian citizen or permanent resident
- Temporary residents with 12+ months remaining
- Valid Australian driver's license required

## Vehicle Requirements

### New Vehicles
- Must be less than 12 months old
- Maximum 5,000km on odometer
- Dealer or private sale accepted
- Comprehensive insurance required

### Used Vehicles
- Maximum 12 years old at loan start
- Acceptable condition and mileage
- Professional valuation may be required
- Clear title essential

### Excluded Vehicles
- Salvage or flood-damaged vehicles
- Racing or heavily modified cars
- Commercial vehicles over 4.5 tonnes
- Vehicles without clear title
