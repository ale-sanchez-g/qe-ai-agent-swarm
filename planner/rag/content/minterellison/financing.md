## Overview

When you need to raise or access finance, you may need to develop both short- and long-term strategies to deliver your goals.

You can rely on our pragmatic commercial approach, sophisticated market knowledge and technical expertise across the full spectrum of financial products.

We can assist you with both new and established financial transactions and structures by delivering innovative solutions that incorporate industry-leading financing techniques.

## Services

We provide solutions across the full risk spectrum and the full lifecycle of borrowing or accessing finance:

- **Asset finance**
- **Derivatives and structured finance**
- **Leveraged and acquisition finance**
- **Projects and infrastructure finance**
- **Real estate finance**
- **Restructuring and insolvency**
- **Crowdsourcing**
- **Trade, commodities and export finance**
- **Banking arrangements**
- **Disputes**

## Recent Experience

### Major Transactions

- **CBA as lead arranger and underwriter** - on syndicated facilities to fund amaysim's acquisition of Click Energy Group for AUD$120 million, and refinancing of its existing facilities with Optus

- **Highbridge and Nomura** - as mezzanine lenders to Quadrant Private Equity in acquiring several major gym and fitness chains including Goodlife Australia, Jetts Fitness and Fitness First Australia

- **GSP Energy Pty Ltd** - multiple bilateral funding facilities obtained in context of the scheme of arrangement demerger of Trustpower Limited into two groups of companies

- **AUD$1.05 billion syndicated facilities** (plus an additional $300 million accordion tranche) to the Walker group in relation to the Collins Square precinct in Melbourne

- **Major Australian REIT** - in respect of the issuance of a convertible bond to refinance an existing debt securities issue, allowing the client to expand the quantum and tenor of this component

### Other Notable Transactions

- **Bank syndicate** - in the restructure of the AUD$780 million secured debt facilities of the Lend Lease managed Australian Prime Property Fund Commercial to a 'Common Terms Deed' platform

- **Lending syndicates** including ANZ, WBC, CBA, HSBC, UOB and Citibank on the AUD$1.35 billion term and revolving facilities to the Blackstone Group in connection with a number of commercial, retail, logistics and data centre assets

- **GE Capital Commercial Finance** - on the finance aspects of the sale of its commercial lending and lease business and portfolio to Sankaty Advisers

- **Hertz** - in relation to an OpCo/FleetCo securitisation structure. The matter was a highly complex restructure of an existing facility involving conforming US securitisation and structural considerations into an Australian deal structure

- **UnitingCare Queensland** - in respect of the State's first social impact bond for the provision of social housing to the Aboriginal and Torres Strait Islander community
