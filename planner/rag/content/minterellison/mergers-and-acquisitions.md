### Pre Deal Services

We understand that doing a deal can be one of the most complex and risky business propositions you can undertake. The potential for error is high in an environment where speed is critical and there is pressure for results.

Our focus is on helping you optimise your outcomes from the very start – from the initial strategic assessment of the transaction, to identifying key drivers, improving deal structures, mitigating risks and challenging assumptions about future performance.

#### Pre Deal Solutions Include:
- Risk and market analysis, risk allocation and strategic planning
- Deal structuring, integration and separation synergies including IP, employee, technology and tax implications
- Understanding changes on corporate governance, directors' and controlling shareholders duties and liabilities
- Understanding the impact of required regulatory processes including competition, licensing, tax and foreign investment issues
- Market engagement, bid process and competition strategies
- Process, due diligence, technology, data room solutions and choosing trusted advisers

### Raising Capital

Companies contemplating doing a deal often need to seek out alternative financing options for their transaction. There are a number of options available, each with its own advantages, risks, fees, and commitments.

Our transactional specialists will work with you to help you navigate the capital road map and optimise your funding arrangements.

#### Capital Raising Solutions Include:
- Planning, financing strategy, including funding option identification and evaluation
- Special investment vehicles for venture capital, private equity or specialised funds
- Understanding the risks and processes of equity capital markets, initial public offerings and listing on a stock exchange
- Preparing for "life after listing", including corporate change management, directors' liabilities, regulatory obligations
- Understanding debt capital market transactions, including MTN programmes, convertible bond issues and subordinated debt
