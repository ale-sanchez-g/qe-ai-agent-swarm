## Overview

Risk is ubiquitous. Its impact can be disastrous and long-lasting. There's no 'one size fits all' solution when it comes to risk. Risk is our business. We approach risk by getting to know your business, identifying potential risks and threats, and developing strategies to mitigate risk and ensure you're protected.

## How We Can Help

We provide solutions across the full risk spectrum and the full lifecycle of business risk management and insurance (from non-contentious to contentious):

### Risk Management Services
- **Risk analysis, compliance and audits**
- **Risk mitigation strategies, training, business continuity, procedure development, and use of risk management tools**
- **Disruption and transition caused by automation, technology and business model changes**
- **New and emerging risk factors** relating to cyber security, climate change and sustainability, class actions and corporate crime
- **Risks relating to regulatory compliance** especially in product liability, occupational health and safety, and workers compensation
- **External political and economic risks** resulting from recession, sanctions, trade changes and terrorism

### Insurance Services
- **Insurance and self-insurance options** across directors and officers insurance, indemnity, property, asset, and other specialist sector insurance needs
- **Reviewing insurance policies and contracts, negotiations terms, reinsurance and claims**
- **Incident response, crisis management and claims**
- **Policy termination and disputes**
